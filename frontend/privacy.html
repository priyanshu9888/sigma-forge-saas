Privacy policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

We designed FigmaForge to be privacy-first. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

We collect your email, billing data (through Stripe), and usage metrics needed to enforce plan limits.

How we use data

We use data to provide the service, secure accounts, and improve performance. We do not sell your data.

Figma content

Design data is fetched only to generate code and is not stored beyond the generation session.

Retention

Generated zip files are stored temporarily for downloads and are deleted automatically.

Contact

If you have questions, reach us at hello@figmaforge.app.
